Bound relay-reconnect replay (#105). Restored subscriptions now advance their `since` to the newest sane `created_at` seen minus a 30-minute guard (`RESUBSCRIBE_SINCE_GUARD_SECONDS`), so a relay reconnect re-requests only that window instead of the whole session; filters without `since` are unchanged. Previously, whole-session replay re-dispatched every stored request whose event id had aged out of the dedup cache, re-executing completed tool calls without any server restart. The transports' `seenEventIds` cache now holds 40,000 entries (`DEFAULT_DEDUP_CACHE_SIZE`, ~20,000 encrypted requests) to cover the replay window. This also corrects the earlier claim that transport dedup fully protects against re-delivery on relay reconnects: suppression stays best-effort and bounded, requests from senders whose clocks trail the newest event by more than the guard may be dropped on reconnect, and same-event transparent retries (CEP-8) older than the guard may not be re-observed after a reconnect.
